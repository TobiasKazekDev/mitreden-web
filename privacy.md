## In short

Mitreden does not collect any personal data. There is no user account, no advertising, no tracking and no analytics. The texts shown stay on your device.

## Microphone and speech recognition

When you start a recording, Mitreden uses your device's microphone and Apple's speech recognition (the Speech framework of iOS and iPadOS) to convert speech into text. For this, the recording may be sent to and processed by Apple. We as the provider of the app receive neither the recording nor the recognized text.

Apple's privacy policy applies to this processing: <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>

Mitreden does not store the recording. The microphone and speech recognition are only used after you have granted permission. You can withdraw your permission at any time in your device settings.

## Data stored on your device

Mitreden only stores the following on your device:

- the last 10 displayed texts (history),
- your settings (live mode, font size, rotated display).

This data does not leave your device through the app. You can delete the history at any time in the app via “Clear history”; deleting the app removes all data.

## Sharing of data

Apart from the speech recognition by Apple described above, Mitreden does not share any data with third parties.

## This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address to deliver the page. More information: <https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement>

## Your rights

As we do not process any personal data about you, we hold no data to which requests for access, correction or deletion could apply. If you have questions, please contact us at the email address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

## Changes

We will update this privacy policy if the app changes. The version published here applies.
